# IIVCS Feedback System

**Operational Feedback | Organizational Feedback | Strategic Reassessment**

The [Insheights Integrated Value Creation System (IIVCS)](README.md) feedback system is how the organization continuously refines where and how it creates value — surfacing signals from the customer journey, translating them into organizational outcomes, and returning them upstream to drive strategic reassessment. Feedback operates at two levels: operational, through [Customer Value Delivery Model (CVDM)](README.md#cvdm--customer-value-delivery-model)'s five feedback loops; and organizational, through the 8 Values metrics that surface outcomes back to [Organization Strategy Model (OSM)](README.md#osm--organization-strategy-model).

---

## Two Levels, and the Bridge Between Them

**Operational feedback** surfaces at the journey and value stream level — CVDM's five continuous feedback loops capture signals from customer experience, journey insights, delivery learning, performance optimization, and strategic signals. These loops return signal to the product team and [Product Transformation System (PTS)](README.md#pts--product-transformation-system) about how value is being delivered and received, and strategic signals to OSM.

**Organizational feedback** surfaces at the organizational level — the 8 Values metrics aggregate the outcomes of strategy, transformation, and delivery into organizational dimensions that feed back into OSM's Governance & Measurement and strategic reassessment.

**Behavioral feedback** bridges the two — AER (Adoption, Engagement, Retention) translates the operational signals into the behavioral patterns of customers and teams that become organizational outcomes. It is the layer that connects how value is delivered (operational) to what the organization generates (organizational). See [AER Behavioral Measurement Model](aer.md).

Together, the levels form the complete upstream feedback path. Viewed through the frameworks it runs CVDM → PTS → OSM; viewed through the measurement layers that ride it, CVDM's loops → AER → 8 Values → OSM — the same flow, two lenses.

## Operational Feedback — CVDM

CVDM's five continuous feedback loops are the operational feedback mechanism of the IIVCS — surfacing signals across Efficiency, Experience, and Effectiveness at the journey and value stage level.

This is the one table of the five loops; the [CVDM Practitioners Guide](guides/cvdm-practitioners-guide.md) links here.

The product team owns every loop. A loop is active when it names the role in the team that acts on it, the decision it informs, and how its signal is collected, with a rough cost, and has informed at least one decision; until then it is planned. A research or analytics team may collect a loop's signal as a capability the product team consumes; the loop and its decision stay with the product team.

| Feedback Loop | What It Surfaces | Typical signal source | Decision it informs | Returns to | Typical cadence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Experience feedback | How the customer felt at each journey step — the first and most immediate signal | The customer's own words: in-product surveys, interviews | Which journey step to examine next | Team / PTS | Collected as it arrives / read weekly |
| Journey insights | Where friction, gaps, and misalignments exist across the journey | The customer's behavior (drop-off, usage analytics) or words | Which value stage to fix | Team / PTS | Sprint / bi-weekly |
| Delivery learning | Whether value stages are delivering intended customer outcomes | Realized customer outcomes | Whether a value stage is retuned, and what PTS changes in the operating model | PTS | Monthly / quarterly |
| Performance optimization | Whether capabilities and processes are performing at the required maturity | Capability and process data | Which capability or process to fix | PTS | Sprint / monthly |
| Strategic signals | Whether strategic priorities remain valid given delivery outcomes | The other four loops, consolidated by the product team | Hypothesis status and priority decisions at OSM's decision forum | OSM | Quarterly / annually |

The cadences shown are typical starting points; run each loop at the organization's own cadence. Each loop reads at least once before the decision it informs — Delivery learning and Performance optimization before PTS's operating-model review, Strategic signals before each OSM decision forum. The out-of-cycle triggers stay. Every loop that reads the customer — Experience feedback, Journey insights, and Delivery learning — draws on at least one source in the customer's own words or behavior. Customer-facing staff add to that signal and do not replace it; their reports are recorded as staff observation. Where no customer-sourced method can be funded yet, the loop may run on staff observation, labelled provisional, and the CVDM System Health Check reads it At Risk until customer signal arrives.

The loops form a diagnostic sequence, each narrowing the cause one layer further. Experience feedback surfaces first — the most immediate signal from the customer journey. Journey insights locate where in the journey the problem sits. Delivery learning and performance optimization work as a pair: delivery learning finds the value stage that is not delivering its outcome, and performance optimization fixes the capability or process behind it. Strategic signals surface last — whether strategic priorities remain valid given delivery outcomes. The product team consolidates its learnings, including these signals, and returns them to OSM, which decides through strategic reassessment.

## Organizational Feedback — 8 Values

The [8 Values](8-values.md) metrics aggregate organizational outcomes and return them upstream to OSM's Governance & Measurement — validating whether strategic priorities are generating the right organizational value across all eight dimensions.

| 8 Value | What It Signals |
|---|---|
| **Customer Value** | Whether strategic customer priorities are generating adoption, engagement, and retention |
| **Financial Value** | Whether strategic investment is generating current and forward-looking financial returns |
| **People Value** | Whether the operating model is building the human capability and engagement the system needs |
| **Operational Value** | Whether the product operating model is performing at the required capability and execution quality |
| **Market Value** | Whether strategic direction is building the brand, reputation, and market position intended |
| **Strategic Value** | Whether strategy formulation is clear, executable, and generating the outcomes hypothesized |
| **Innovation Value** | Whether investment in future value streams is adequate and generating the pipeline needed |
| **Impact Value** | Whether the organization is advancing its mission and living its values through delivery |

People Value and Operational Value feed PTS — signaling whether the operating model needs adjustment. All other Values feed OSM — directly informing Governance & Measurement and strategic reassessment.

## The Complete Feedback Path

The IIVCS feedback system runs upstream in two layers — operational signals rising first, organizational signals confirming last.

```
CVDM      Experience → Journey insights → Delivery learning → Performance optimization → Strategic signals
                ↓
AER       Adoption → Engagement → Retention            (two tracks: Customer AER and Organizational AER)
                ↓
8 Values  People · Operational · Customer · Financial · Market · Strategic · Innovation · Impact
                ↓                                                   ↓
          People + Operational Value                      Customer · Financial · Market ·
          → PTS (operating-model adjustment)              Strategic · Innovation · Impact
                                                                    ↓
                                                          OSM  Governance & Measurement → Strategic Reassessment
```

The path forks at the 8 Values: People and Operational Value feed PTS to signal whether the operating model needs adjustment; the other six feed OSM's Governance & Measurement and strategic reassessment. AER runs as two parallel tracks — Customer AER and Organizational AER.

[3E](3e.md) is the first layer of the measurement language at every point in the return journey — grounding both operational and organizational feedback in Efficiency, Experience, and Effectiveness, and passing it on to [AER](aer.md) and the [8 Values](8-values.md).

The feedback runs upstream — CVDM to PTS to OSM — customer signals becoming delivery learning, delivery learning becoming strategic signals, continuously refining where the organization creates value.

---

## Summary

The IIVCS feedback system ensures the organization never loses contact with reality — at the journey level or the organizational level.

Operational feedback keeps PTS connected to customer experience and delivery quality. Organizational feedback keeps OSM connected to the outcomes its strategic priorities are — or are not — generating. Together they form the continuous learning loop that makes the IIVCS a living system, not a static framework.
