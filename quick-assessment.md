# IIVCS Quick Self-Assessment

**A directional reading of your [Integrated Value Creation System (IIVCS)](README.md) adoption maturity in a few minutes — one question per element.**

---

This is the fast self-orientation tool for the [Adoption Sequencing & Maturity Model](adoption-maturity.md). For a rigorous, scored evaluation that produces a full maturity profile, use the IIVCS Detailed Assessment.

For each IIVCS element, pick the **highest statement that is *fully* true** today. Each maps to a maturity level: **a = Early, b = Developing, c = Mature.** If a statement is only partly true, you have not reached that level — pick the one below. (This mirrors the gated scoring in the Detailed Assessment: you earn a level by clearing it, not by getting close.)

> **Assess one value stream at a time.** IIVCS maturity is about how the frameworks connect to deliver value — and they connect *on a value stream*. Answer for a single, named value stream, not the whole organization at once.

> **Who answers it.** A single leader can run this quick check for a directional read — but having a few people (strategy, operating model, delivery, measurement) **answer independently and then compare** gives a truer answer: each role sees only its own layer, and where their answers diverge is itself a finding (see [When several people answer](#when-several-people-answer)). For the rigorous version of this — independent scoring, evidence, and gated levels with defined roles — use the Detailed Assessment.

---

## The Questions

**1. [Organization Strategy Model (OSM)](README.md#osm--organization-strategy-model) — Strategy & Governance.** How are your strategic priorities expressed and governed?
- **a)** As initiatives or activities in a plan; not governed by value stream
- **b)** As outcomes mapped to some value streams; reviewed but rarely revised
- **c)** As value-stream-governed outcomes grounded in explicit choices (where to play, how to win) and written assumptions, revised on dual-track [Adoption, Engagement, Retention (AER)](aer.md) + 8 Values signal

**2. [Product Transformation System (PTS)](README.md#pts--product-transformation-system) — Operating Model.** How are delivery teams organized?
- **a)** Project teams by function, assembled and disbanded per initiative, with handoffs
- **b)** Some persistent cross-functional teams on key value streams; partial enabler activation
- **c)** Persistent value-stream teams; all four [Product Operating Model (POM)](glossary.md#core-system-and-frameworks) Enablers active; Organizational AER measured

**3. [Customer Value Delivery Model (CVDM)](README.md#cvdm--customer-value-delivery-model) — Delivery & Realization.** How is delivery organized relative to the customer journey?
- **a)** By internal process; there is no journey-to-value-stream map
- **b)** Key journeys mapped to value streams; some value stages defined; a few loops running
- **c)** Full journey mapping; value stages defined as outcomes; five loops active; Customer AER measured

**4. [Efficiency, Experience, Effectiveness (3E)](3e.md) — Operational Measurement.** How do you measure whether value is delivered at a given element?
- **a)** Activity or output metrics; no consistent measurement language
- **b)** 3E applied at some elements; Use → Feel → Achieve read in places
- **c)** 3E applied consistently across levels and read in sequence diagnostically

**5. AER — Behavioral Measurement.** How do you measure behavioral response — adoption, engagement, retention?
- **a)** Not measured beyond satisfaction surveys
- **b)** One track (usually Customer) measured on some journeys
- **c)** Dual-track (Customer + Organizational) measured in parallel and reconciled at OSM

**6. 8 Values — Organizational Outcomes.** How do you measure the value the organization generates?
- **a)** Financial and operational only, in silos
- **b)** Several dimensions tracked; some traceable to an operational source
- **c)** All 8 Values aggregated from their operational source, feeding OSM governance

**7. Feedback System — Upstream Loops.** What happens to customer and delivery signal?
- **a)** It dies at the team; there is no upstream feedback
- **b)** Operational loops run and reach PTS; signal informs delivery
- **c)** Full upstream path CVDM → PTS → OSM drives strategic reassessment

**8. Product Mindset — Foundation.** What best describes how teams work?
- **a)** Output-focused project culture; assumptions go untested
- **b)** Outcome language adopted; discovery practiced in places
- **c)** Customer obsession, outcome-over-output, continuous discovery, hypothesis-driven thinking, and cross-functional ownership are the norm

## Read Your Result

1. **Your system level comes from the chain.** Questions 1, 2, 3 and 7 are the chain strategy → operating model → delivery → feedback. Your lowest answer among them is your level: **a** → *Early*, **b** → *Developing*, **c** → *Mature*, as in the [Maturity Model](adoption-maturity.md#maturity-across-the-suite). It is not an average: high answers elsewhere do not raise it.
2. **Read questions 4, 5, 6 and 8 as enablers.** They cover the measurement and the working culture the frameworks depend on. An enabler answered below your level is a finding: it limits how far the frameworks it supports can climb.
3. **Keep your eight answers as your profile**: the four chain answers, then the four enablers (for example: chain b a b a; enablers b b a b). Mixed answers are the most common result, and the most useful: the spread shows which connections are missing.
4. **Find your break and your starting point.** Your break is your lowest chain answer: the point where value leaks out of the system, and your adoption priority. The Maturity Model's [From an assessment result](adoption-maturity.md#from-an-assessment-result) gives the tie rule, the entry point the break points to, and what to do when there is no break.

### When several people answer

1. **Answer privately first.** Each person answers all eight questions alone, for the same value stream, before anyone compares.
2. **Line the answers up**, question by question.
3. **Treat every difference as a finding**, and note which roles gave which answer. People who run the stream day to day and people who govern it often see it differently, and that gap is worth knowing.
4. **Take the lower answer**, unless someone shows the group something it can all check, such as a document, a dashboard or a decision on record. Agreement reached by discussion alone does not count.
5. **Read your result** as above, from the answers that stand.

> **Want the full picture?** This quick check is directional only — a one-question read per element, no evidence test. The IIVCS Detailed Assessment applies evidence-anchored, gated criteria across all eight components and produces a maturity profile with strengths, constraint, and adoption priority. Use this to orient; use the detailed assessment to decide.

> **What a level means.** Maturity here is **adoption fidelity** — the frameworks present and connected — not proof of value created. A Mature reading means the machinery is in place and wired together, not that the organization is yet creating more value.
