# Insheights AER Behavioral Measurement Model — Practitioners Guide

**How to set up and read Adoption, Engagement, Retention (AER) on one value stream, across both tracks, in the [Insheights Integrated Value Creation System (IIVCS)](../README.md).**

---

## At a Glance

| | |
|---|---|
| **What it does** | Sets up Organizational AER on one value stream and reads it against Customer AER for the same stream. Organizational AER asks whether teams adopt, engage with and keep the operating model. Customer AER asks whether customers adopt, engage with and stay with the value. The result is one behavioral reading that says which track is breaking and what to do about it |
| **When to use it** | Once an operating model is being stood up on a value stream ([Product Transformation System (PTS)](../README.md#pts--product-transformation-system), [Step 5 of its guide](pts-practitioners-guide.md#step-5--activate-the-feedback-and-learning-loop)); or when a value stream's [Efficiency, Experience, Effectiveness (3E)](../3e.md) or [8 Values](../8-values.md) reading moves and you need to know whether behavior moved first |
| **Who runs it** | The value-stream owner |
| **Who must be in the room** | Value-stream owner; the executive sponsor at the start and at any handover; Product Operating Model (POM) Leadership; each team's accountable product role; the person who owns Customer AER instrumentation for the stream; a people or human resources (HR) lead, if team continuity and turnover are to be read |
| **Key inputs** | A value stream with an operating model being stood up; a named executive sponsor and value-stream owner; team artifacts and review records; the stream's Customer AER instrumentation from the [Customer Value Delivery Model (CVDM)](../README.md#cvdm--customer-value-delivery-model) |
| **Key outputs** | One named Organizational AER signal per stage, each with a baseline and a paired check on the work; a two-track reading for the value stream with one action; the review cadence, owners and a handover rule |
| **Typical timeframe** | A recommendation; the organization's planning cadence sets it. First reading: one working session once the records are gathered. Then at each operating-model review. The [coverage bar](../metrics-and-indicators.md#reference-bars-for-system-maturity) needs at least three reviews, since practice persistence first reads at the second: about three quarters at a quarterly cadence |

> **New to the IIVCS?** This guide uses system terms — value stream, operating model, POM Enablers, 3E, the 8 Values, [Organization Strategy Model (OSM)](../README.md#osm--organization-strategy-model), PTS, CVDM — defined in the [Glossary](../glossary.md). The model itself is set out in [AER](../aer.md); read it first if you are starting here.

> **Not a product organization?** "Product team" → "delivery team"; "customer" → "patient/client/citizen/beneficiary"; "operating model" → "way of working." See [Translation Notes](#translation-notes).

> **Where this fits:** AER is the behavioral layer of the measurement architecture, between 3E and the 8 Values. Organizational AER is measured in PTS; Customer AER is instrumented in CVDM. This guide sets up the first and reads the two together.

## Before You Start

**Inputs required**

- [ ] **One value stream** with an operating model being stood up on it — the pilot stream, if PTS started with one ([PTS Practitioners Guide](pts-practitioners-guide.md#before-you-start))
- [ ] **A named executive sponsor and value-stream owner**, as the PTS guide defines them
- [ ] **Team artifacts and review records** for the stream's teams: who holds the accountable product role, outcome goals and their baselines, discovery records, operating-model review notes, and the decisions that cite them
- [ ] **Customer AER for the same stream**, instrumented in [CVDM Step 4](cvdm-practitioners-guide.md#step-4--instrument-3e-and-customer-aer-across-the-journey), or the analytics the organization already keeps
- [ ] **Team rosters and turnover data** by capability area, if Retention is to be read beyond practice persistence

**People required**

| Role | Why they must be present |
|---|---|
| Value-stream owner | Reads the signals and acts on them, with POM Leadership; owns retuning the operating model when adoption or engagement drops |
| Executive sponsor | Holds the mandate the way of working depends on; present at the start and at any handover |
| Each team's accountable product role | Knows which practices run, and where the artifacts overstate them |
| Customer AER owner | Brings the customer-side reading for the same stream; does not re-instrument it here |
| People / HR lead, if turnover is read | Supplies team continuity and voluntary turnover, read by capability area |

**Prepare the room**

- **Without a named sponsor, do not start.** Use the [Adoption Sequencing & Maturity Model](../adoption-maturity.md#before-you-start) to find one first.
- Gather the last two quarters of review notes and decision records before the session. Expect them to be uneven; that is a finding, not a reason to wait.
- Add no new forum. The reading is held at the existing operating-model review ([Step 6](#step-6--hold-the-reading-at-the-operating-model-review)).

## How to Run It

Each step below is an action. Complete them in order — later steps depend on earlier outputs. Steps 2 and 3 set up Organizational AER; Step 4 brings in Customer AER; Step 5 reads the two together.

---

### Step 1 — Choose the value stream and name who acts

**What you do:** Name the value stream, its executive sponsor and its value-stream owner, and the operating-model review at which the reading will be held.

**Key question this step answers:** *Whose way of working are we reading, and who acts when it slips?*

**Inputs:** The value stream the operating model is stood up on; the PTS roles
**Output:** The value stream, its named sponsor and owner, and the review the reading goes to

**Guidance:**
- Start with one value stream: the one the operating model is stood up on first. Widen only once its reading reaches a decision.
- Name who acts, not who receives. Organizational AER that no leader acts on is a dashboard ([PTS Step 5](pts-practitioners-guide.md#step-5--activate-the-feedback-and-learning-loop)).
- You are done when you can say: "[Owner] reads AER for [value stream] at [review], under [sponsor]'s mandate."

---

### Step 2 — Name one Organizational AER signal per stage and baseline it

**What you do:** For each stage, pick one signal from [Metrics and Indicators](../metrics-and-indicators.md#organizational-aer), read it from the team artifacts and review records, and record it as the baseline.

**Key question this step answers:** *What do the records say teams do with the way of working today?*

**Inputs:** The value stream and its review (Step 1); team artifacts and review records
**Output:** One named signal per stage, each with a first reading, labelled partial where records are thin

**Guidance:**
- Start with **practice coverage** (Adoption), **decision trace and team-made change** (Engagement) and **practice persistence** (Retention). Coverage reads four practices in each team's artifacts: a named accountable product role, outcome goals with baselines, discovery records, and operating-model reviews held. Add team continuity and voluntary turnover to Retention when the data is there, and read them with the operating model's 3E Effectiveness ([AER](../aer.md#the-two-tracks)).
- Do not use an engagement survey for Engagement. It records what people say, not what they do, and belongs to 3E Experience ([Limitations](../aer.md#limitations)).
- Practice persistence needs two reviews. At the first, record it as **unmeasured**, not as a pass.
- Record the first reading as the baseline. The normal range, per [the change rule](../metrics-and-indicators.md#when-a-reading-has-changed), needs the spread of readings over a baseline period, so it builds up over later reviews; until then, no reading counts as a change. There are no external benchmarks; compare to your own baseline.
- You are done when each stage has a named signal and a first reading, or is recorded as unmeasured, and each partial reading says what is missing.

---

### Step 3 — Pair each artifact reading with a check on the work

**What you do:** Beside each Adoption and persistence reading, name one behavioral signal from the work itself that confirms the practice runs. For Engagement, whose signal is the decision trace itself, check that the cited decisions changed what the team then did.

**Key question this step answers:** *Is the practice running, or are only its artifacts being produced?*

**Inputs:** The signals and first readings (Step 2); decision records; backlogs and priority records
**Output:** Each artifact reading with its paired check, marked confirmed or not confirmed

**Guidance:**
- A team can produce the artifacts without running the practice ([Limitations](../aer.md#limitations)). Good checks: the practice cited in a recorded decision; a backlog or priority that changed after a discovery finding.
- A reading whose check fails is **not confirmed**. Report it that way at the review; do not count it toward practice coverage.
- Keep one check per reading. Several weak checks are not one strong one.
- You are done when every Adoption and persistence reading, and every cited decision, is marked confirmed or not confirmed.

---

### Step 4 — Bring in the Customer AER reading for the same stream

**What you do:** Take one Customer AER signal per stage for the same value stream, as instrumented in [CVDM Step 4](cvdm-practitioners-guide.md#step-4--instrument-3e-and-customer-aer-across-the-journey), with its baseline.

**Key question this step answers:** *What are customers doing with the value this stream delivers?*

**Inputs:** CVDM's Customer AER instrumentation, or the analytics already kept; the signals in [Metrics and Indicators](../metrics-and-indicators.md#customer-aer)
**Output:** One Customer AER reading per stage for the stream, or the stage recorded as unmeasured

**Guidance:**
- This step reads Customer AER; it does not instrument it. A stage with no signal is recorded as **unmeasured** and goes back to CVDM Step 4.
- Customer analytics are usually kept already, but check that the signal is behavioral and covers this stream, not the whole customer base.
- Where the relationship is one-off, Retention may not exist for most customers. Read Recommendation, or Retention for the segment that does return, and say which ([AER](../aer.md#the-two-tracks)).
- You are done when each stage has a Customer AER reading with its baseline, or is recorded as unmeasured and sent back to CVDM Step 4.

---

### Step 5 — Read the two tracks against each other and against 3E

**What you do:** Put the Organizational and Customer readings side by side, stage by stage, and read each against its 3E dimension. Name where behavior is breaking, on which track, and one action.

**Key question this step answers:** *Which track is breaking, at which stage, and does 3E explain it?*

**Inputs:** Confirmed Organizational AER readings (Steps 2–3); Customer AER readings (Step 4); the operating model's 3E (PTS) and the journey's 3E (CVDM)
**Output:** A two-track reading for the value stream: the breaking stage and track, the 3E reading beside it, and one named action with its owner

**Guidance:**
- **Read the tracks together.** Both are expected to be necessary ([AER](../aer.md#the-two-tracks)). A reading **falls** when it shows a counted decline under [the change rule](../metrics-and-indicators.md#when-a-reading-has-changed), or sits below its at-risk floor; it **holds** otherwise. A first reading has no earlier one to fall from:

  | Organizational AER | Customer AER | Read it as | Look first at |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Baseline only | Either | No trend yet on the organizational side; read the level against the bar, and the customer track as below | Whether the low stages are partial records or practices not running |
  | Holds | Holds | Behavior supports the value; confirm with 3E Effectiveness and the 8 Values | Whether the 8 Values follow over time |
  | Holds | Falls | The way of working runs, but customers do not respond | The journey's 3E in CVDM, then the value proposition |
  | Falls | Holds | Current results are expected to rest on practices that are dropping out; the customer fall, if it comes, comes later | The operating model's 3E in PTS; sponsorship |
  | Falls | Falls | The delivery system is not carrying the stream | The operating model's 3E Effectiveness, then sponsorship |

- **Read each stage with its 3E dimension**, as [How 3E Drives AER](../aer.md#how-3e-drives-aer) sets out: Efficiency with Adoption, Experience with Engagement, Effectiveness with Retention. A fall that follows a weak 3E dimension is the expected chain; fix the 3E cause. A fall while 3E holds points elsewhere — for Organizational Retention, to a change of sponsor or owner, or to the labor market. Where the operating model has no 3E reading yet, record it as unmeasured and send it back to PTS; do not read the organizational track's cause from the journey's 3E.
- **Where a customer movement confirms an organizational reading**, say so: the two are then not independent evidence.
- **Read Adoption and Engagement first.** They lead; Retention lags and confirms. Do not wait for Retention to act.
- These links are the model's reasoned hypotheses, not measured laws. [AER](../aer.md#how-3e-drives-aer) gives each one a condition for revisiting it; record a contrary reading rather than explaining it away.
- The output is an action, not an observation. "Engagement is low" is not complete. "Reviews are held but cited in no decision — action: the owner brings the survey-booking finding to the next review as a decision" is.
- You are done when the reading names the breaking stage and track, the 3E reading beside it, and one action with its owner.

---

### Step 6 — Hold the reading at the operating-model review

**What you do:** Read AER at each operating-model review, name who acts on each signal, set out-of-cycle triggers, and set a handover rule for a change of sponsor or owner.

**Key question this step answers:** *How will we know when the way of working is slipping, and who acts?*

**Inputs:** The two-track reading (Step 5); the PTS review cadence; the CVDM feedback loops
**Output:** Review cadence, named owners, out-of-cycle triggers, a handover rule, and the return path

**Guidance:**
- **Cadence:** the value-stream operating-model review, held at the organization's planning cadence before each OSM decision forum so its read reaches it ([PTS Step 5](pts-practitioners-guide.md#step-5--activate-the-feedback-and-learning-loop)).
- **Owners:** the value-stream owner, with POM Leadership, acts on Organizational AER. The Customer AER owner brings the customer reading. One owner per signal.
- **Out-of-cycle triggers:** a practice dropping out of team artifacts between reviews, which each team's accountable product role watches and raises with the value-stream owner; a change of executive sponsor or value-stream owner, which the value-stream owner raises.
- **Handover rule:** when the sponsor or owner changes, the incoming one restates the mandate before the next review, and Organizational Retention is read at that review as a sponsorship risk ([Limitations](../aer.md#limitations)). If the mandate has not been restated by then, the value-stream owner takes the reading to the OSM decision forum, out of cycle if needed. The forum confirms the mandate or names a sponsor, and the stream goes back to Step 1.
- **Return path:** Organizational AER through PTS's operating-model review to OSM; Customer AER through CVDM's loops ([IIVCS Feedback System](../feedback.md)).
- You are done when the value stream meets the **Organizational AER coverage** bar in [Metrics and Indicators](../metrics-and-indicators.md#reference-bars-for-system-maturity).

## Worked Example

> **Illustrative.** This example is a composite written to show the method, before any field run. It is not a client case, and its numbers are not benchmarks. It is deliberately untidy: real first readings usually are.

> **Scenario:** A regional utility is moving its New Connections value stream — households and builders asking for a new supply — from a project structure to four persistent teams. Applicants increasingly call to chase progress. Two quarterly operating-model reviews have been held; the value-stream owner first reads AER at the third.

**Step 1 — Choose the value stream and name who acts**

Value stream: **New Connections**. Executive sponsor: the chief operating officer. Value-stream owner: the head of New Connections. The reading goes to the quarterly operating-model review, held before the investment committee (the OSM decision forum).

**Step 2 — Name one signal per stage and baseline it**

| Stage | Signal | First reading |
|---|---|---|
| Adoption | Practice coverage | 2 of 4 teams show all four practices in their artifacts. One team keeps no review notes — **partial** |
| Engagement | Decision trace and team-made change | 1 of the 2 earlier reviews is cited in a recorded decision; no team-made change |
| Retention | Practice persistence | **Unmeasured** until the next review, the second at which AER is read |

**Step 3 — Pair each artifact reading with a check**

One of the two covered teams shows a discovery record for every sprint, but no recorded decision cites a discovery finding and its backlog did not change after any of them. That team's coverage is **not confirmed**. Confirmed coverage: 1 of 4 teams.

**Step 4 — Bring in Customer AER**

From the customer analytics already kept: Adoption, applications started online, holds against baseline. Engagement, applicants who book their site survey without a call, has fallen outside its normal range for three readings — a counted decline. Retention does not exist for most households, which connect once; the team reads repeat applications from builders instead, and says so.

**Step 5 — Read the two tracks together**

The customer track falls at Engagement, and the organizational track is baseline only: there is no earlier reading for it to fall from. The journey's 3E shows the cause on the customer side: after applying, applicants cannot see where their application stands and call to chase it, and booking the survey takes one of those calls (Experience weak at that value stage, which is expected to show in Engagement). On the organizational side, the operating model's 3E has a first reading from PTS, and the review that should have caught the fall is not reaching decisions. Action: the value-stream owner brings the survey-booking finding to the next review as a named decision, and the team whose coverage was not confirmed reviews how it uses its discovery records.

**Step 6 — Hold the reading at the review**

At the next review, practice persistence reads for the first time. Between the reviews the chief operating officer moves to another role, and a new sponsor is named. Under the handover rule the new sponsor restates the mandate before the review. Persistence has dropped in two teams. One reading is a prompt to look, not a counted change. The operating model's 3E Effectiveness holds, so the owner reads the drop as a sponsorship risk, as the handover rule says, and takes it to the new sponsor. Online survey booking is in the backlog, cited in a recorded decision. The team whose coverage was not confirmed now brings its discovery findings to each review as agenda items, a change it proposed and made itself: the stream's first team-made change to the way of working.

> **What it produced:**
> - A first Organizational AER reading: one named signal per stage, labelled partial, with one artifact reading not confirmed
> - A two-track reading that put the customer-side Engagement fall and the organizational Engagement gap side by side
> - A handover rule that was used once, at the next review
> - Not yet the coverage bar: practice persistence has been read at one review, and the bar needs each stage read at two or more consecutive reviews. The recorded changes are already in place, so it can be met at the next review, when persistence reads for the second time

## Measurement Connection

AER takes 3E in and gives the 8 Values out.

### 3E going in

Each AER stage is read with its 3E dimension, on the track it belongs to: the operating model's 3E for Organizational AER ([PTS Practitioners Guide](pts-practitioners-guide.md#3e-application)), the journey's 3E for Customer AER ([CVDM Practitioners Guide](cvdm-practitioners-guide.md#3e-application)). The expected links, and what would count against each, are in [How 3E Drives AER](../aer.md#how-3e-drives-aer).

### 8 Values coming out

Customer AER is expected to drive Customer and Financial Value; Organizational AER, People Value; both compound into the others. The links are in [How AER Drives the 8 Values](../aer.md#how-aer-drives-the-8-values), and the [8 Values Practitioners Guide](8-values-practitioners-guide.md) reads the two tracks from here.

## System Health Check

These signals confirm AER is being read and acted on — not just recorded. Review them at each operating-model review.

| Signal | Healthy | At Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Stage coverage | Every stage on both tracks has a named behavioral signal with a baseline | Stages left unmeasured without saying so, or filled with survey scores |
| Paired checks | Each artifact reading has its check, and unconfirmed readings are reported as such | Coverage counted from artifacts alone |
| Two tracks, one reading | Both tracks are read for the same value stream at the same review | The tracks are read in different forums and never compared |
| Readings reach decisions | A named owner acts, and readings lead to recorded changes, as the [coverage bar](../metrics-and-indicators.md#reference-bars-for-system-maturity) asks | Readings are reported, and nothing changes |
| Sponsorship continuity | A change of sponsor or owner triggers the handover rule | A change goes unremarked until practices have dropped out |

**If two or more signals are At Risk:** stop adding signals. Go back to Step 1, confirm the sponsor and owner, and take the reading to the executive sponsor before the next review.

| Customer | Patient / client | Citizen / resident | Client | End customer / buyer | Client / policyholder |

> **Core principle:** Adoption, Engagement and Retention read the same way in every sector — what people do, on both tracks, read together. Only the names of the records and the forums change.

## Quick Reference

**Steps**

1. Choose the value stream and name who acts — *Whose way of working, and who acts?*
2. Name one Organizational AER signal per stage — *What do the records show today?*
3. Pair each artifact reading with a check — *Is the practice running?*
4. Bring in Customer AER for the same stream — *What are customers doing?*
5. Read the two tracks against each other and 3E — *Which track breaks, where, and why?*
6. Hold the reading at the operating-model review — *How will we know it slips?*

**Health signals at a glance**

- Every stage on both tracks has a behavioral signal and a baseline
- Every artifact reading has its check
- Both tracks are read together, for one stream, at one review
- Readings lead to recorded changes
- A change of sponsor or owner triggers the handover rule

**Two-track reading**

```
Org holds, Customer falls  → look at the journey's 3E, then the value proposition
Org falls, Customer holds  → look at the operating model's 3E and sponsorship; customers are expected to follow later
Both fall                  → the operating model's Effectiveness first, then sponsorship
```

**Primary 8 Values driven:** Customer Value and Financial Value (Customer AER), People Value (Organizational AER)
